Automatic Street Light Using Arduino UNO Controller

An autonomous energy-conservation system engineered to automate municipal street illumination through photoresistor (LDR) sensor arrays, eliminating daytime energy waste and manual switching.

SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE
LDR Sensor → Voltage Divider → ADC Pin A0 (ATmega328P) → Relay Driver (Pin 13)
Key Engineering Contributions:
  • Sensor Interfacing & Conditioning: Designed a voltage divider circuit interfacing a CdS photoresistor with the 10-bit Analog-to-Digital Converter (ADC) of the ATmega328P microcontroller.
  • Hysteresis Algorithm in Embedded C: Implemented a digital hysteresis threshold band (±25 LSB) in C to completely eliminate high-frequency relay contact bouncing and chattering during twilight transitions.
  • Power Optimization & Safety: Automated optoisolated relay drivers to switch high-voltage lighting loads safely, reducing simulated power consumption by up to 40%.

Verified Prototype

Smart Home Automation Using Arduino UNO Controller

A centralized multi-sensor smart hub combining analog temperature monitoring for automated climate control with infrared (IR) beam monitoring for perimeter security.

SIGNAL ARCHITECTURE
LM35 (A1) & IR (D7) → ATmega328P Processing → AC Relay (Pin 8) & Alarm (Pin 9)
Key Engineering Contributions:
  • Thermal Closed-Loop Regulation: Interfaced an LM35 linear temperature sensor (10mV/°C) with dynamic setpoint logic, triggering the cooling subsystem automatically when temperature exceeds 30.0°C.
  • Infrared Proximity Sensing: Configured digital active-LOW IR reflective sensors on Pin 7 with internal pull-up resistors for immediate object and proximity detection.
  • Electrical Isolation & Actuation: Employed multi-channel relay isolation circuits to decouple the sensitive 5V TTL logic from noisy inductive actuator and appliance loads.
