An automated energy-conservation system designed to intelligently control street lighting through light-dependent resistor (LDR) sensor arrays, eliminating energy wastage during daytime.

Engineering Details & Solutions:

  • Relay Load Control: Automated single-channel optical relay modules to switch high-power illumination loads safely and automatically.
